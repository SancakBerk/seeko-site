Four scans and a checklist

Lens scan

Your flash blinks and the camera looks for small points that blink with it, the way a lens sends light back.

IR scan

The infrared lights of night cameras are invisible to your eyes but show up on most front cameras.

Network scan

Lists the devices on the Wi‑Fi you joined and shows possible cameras with the reasons.

Bluetooth and trackers

Follow a signal to a device, and check whether an AirTag, Tile or SmartTag is travelling with you.

Room checklist

The spots where cameras are often hidden, room by room.

Evidence report

Photos and notes from the room in one PDF for the hotel, the host or the police. With Pro.

Questions

Can an app find every camera?

No phone app can promise that. Using all the scans and the checklist together gives the widest check; the app tells you how to use each one.

Do I need Wi‑Fi?

Only for the network scan. Lens, IR and Bluetooth scans work without it.
