What the app does

Seeko gives you tools to look for signs of hidden cameras: a lens scan, an IR scan, a network scan, a Bluetooth scan, a room checklist and a tracker check. Results are signs that help you decide what to look at more closely; they are not proof that a room has or doesn't have a camera.

Using it well

Use the app only in places you are staying in or are allowed to check, and follow local laws. Don't open, damage or remove other people's devices. If you find something, leave it as it is and contact the host, the platform or the police.

Free use and Pro

Your first three room checks and the tracker check are free. Pro adds unlimited room checks and rescans, Trip Mode, history, PDF reports and evidence reports.

  • Pro Yearly renews automatically each year until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to avoid the next charge.
  • Trip Pass gives Pro for 7 days and ends on its own; it doesn't renew.
  • Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Restore purchases from the paywall or Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
